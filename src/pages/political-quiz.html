Which party do you match?

16 questions on issues and priorities. You'll see where you land on the economic and social scales and how closely your answers match Democrats, Republicans and independents.

How the quiz decides

Each question was asked in national surveys by Pew Research Center or Gallup, so we know roughly how Democrats, Republicans and independents answer it. The quiz compares your full set of answers with each group and reports how likely your answers are to come from each one. Ranking questions compare your priorities with each group's. In a test with 9,000 simulated respondents drawn from those survey patterns, it named the right group 92% of the time; real people are messier than simulations, so treat the result as a match, not a label.

Your answers stay in your browser. They're saved to your account only if you're signed in and choose to save them.
