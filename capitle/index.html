Capitle

Guess the politician of the day. Each guess shows how close you are on party, state, chamber, age and years in office.

GuessPartyStateChamberAgeYears in CongressGender

How to play

You get 8 guesses. Green means a match. Yellow means close: a state in the same region, an age within 3 years, or years in Congress within 3. Arrows point toward the answer.

You get three hints, one from each tier, and you choose which clue to take. Hint 1 (gender, chamber or party) is open until your 2nd guess. Hint 2 (age range or terms served) opens after your 1st guess and closes at your 4th. Hint 3 (state or year they took office) opens after your 3rd guess and closes at your 6th. A new politician appears at midnight Eastern.
